Works / Electroacoustic / A Mechanics of Almost V

Craquelure

Two forms of stored energy — harp-string tension and ceramic brittleness — brought to a region where their behaviours become interchangeable.

Year
2026
Category
Electroacoustic
Instrumentation
Fixed media — harp and ceramic/porcelain objects

Craquelure brings together two forms of stored energy. A harp string is held under tension until a pluck releases its energy into vibration. A ceramic or porcelain surface appears stable until impact, stress or fracture reveals a network of discontinuities. The work searches for a region in which these behaviours become interchangeable.

Fifth piece of A Mechanics of Almost.
