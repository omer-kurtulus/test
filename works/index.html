09 Electroacoustic / A Mechanics of Almost III Intergrain A continuous scraping gesture examined at a much smaller scale — a pick's ridge-by-ridge contact across a string, set against the güiro's related structure at another physical scale. 2026 Fixed media — bass/electric guitar string scrapes and güiro
