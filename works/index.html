06 Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost I 2026 Anaculaton Timpani reduced to impact → membrane → resonance → decay, then interfered with: attacks activate virtual resonating bodies beyond the instrument's scale, alongside wind, tunnels, machinery and distant environmental resonance. 07 Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost V 2026 Craquelure Two forms of stored energy — harp-string tension and ceramic brittleness — brought to a region where their behaviours become interchangeable. Fixed media — harp and ceramic/porcelain objects
08 Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost VI 2026 Endobody The body as an arrangement of internal spaces: vocal cavities and a bass drum's enclosed volume, working from their structural similarity. Fixed media — voice and bass drum
09 Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost III 2026 Intergrain A continuous scraping gesture examined at a much smaller scale — a pick's ridge-by-ridge contact across a string, set against the güiro's related structure at another physical scale. Fixed media — bass/electric guitar string scrapes and güiro
