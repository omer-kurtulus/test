04 Aerolith Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost IV 2026 An air-column instrument beside a resonating metal surface: bass-clarinet breath and multiphonics exchanged with a bowed cymbal's slowly transforming partials. Fixed media — bass clarinet and bowed cymbal
05 Afterglare Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost II 2026 The zurna's penetrating upper spectrum treated as a physical condition: sustained tones extended, upper partials exposed and separated until the instrument's apparent singularity fragments. Fixed media — zurna
06 Anaculaton Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost I 2026 Timpani reduced to impact → membrane → resonance → decay, then interfered with: attacks activate virtual resonating bodies beyond the instrument's scale, alongside wind, tunnels, machinery and distant environmental resonance. Fixed media — timpani and field recordings
07 Craquelure Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost V 2026 Two forms of stored energy — harp-string tension and ceramic brittleness — brought to a region where their behaviours become interchangeable. Fixed media — harp and ceramic/porcelain objects
08 Endobody Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost VI 2026 The body as an arrangement of internal spaces: vocal cavities and a bass drum's enclosed volume, working from their structural similarity. Fixed media — voice and bass drum
09 Intergrain Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost III 2026 A continuous scraping gesture examined at a much smaller scale — a pick's ridge-by-ridge contact across a string, set against the güiro's related structure at another physical scale. Fixed media — bass/electric guitar string scrapes and güiro
10 Remanence Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost VII 2026 What remains after a sound's apparent cause disappears: prepared-piano strings, muted attacks and soundboard resonance, with springs holding excitation that re-emerges later. Fixed media — prepared piano and metal springs
