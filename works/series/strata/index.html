Album · 02 · 2026

Strata

An album on the sonic archaeologies — what survives of a sound after everything it has passed through.

About

Strata is an album in which I research the sonic archaeologies. Both pieces so far excavate Istanbul, and both are dedicated to Halil İbrahim Tutar.

The two are the same excavation carried out by different means. Strata cuts the trench by hand — a 36-channel periphonic field in which every layer sounds from the first second, separated only by height. STRATA II hands the excavation to neural models trained on nothing of the material they erode, and listens to what they cannot carry.
