Album · 07 · 2026

A Mechanics of Almost

Seven fixed-media electroacoustic works, each built from a single source instrument taken to the edge of its physical mechanism — impact, friction, tension, cavity, stored energy — until the instrument is almost gone.

A Mechanics of Almost is a cycle of seven fixed-media electroacoustic works. Each piece takes one source instrument and follows its mechanism — a membrane, a reed, a bowed surface, a string under tension — to the point where the mechanism itself is almost gone, and what remains is the residue of a physical gesture.

The seven works are Anaculaton, Afterglare, Intergrain, Aerolith, Craquelure, Endobody and Remanence.

Released on Bandcamp.

Works in this album

07
№ 01 2026 Anaculaton Electroacoustic Timpani reduced to impact → membrane → resonance → decay, then interfered with: attacks activate virtual resonating bodies beyond the instrument's scale, alongside wind, tunnels, machinery and distant environmental resonance. Fixed media — timpani and field recordings
№ 02 2026 Afterglare Electroacoustic The zurna's penetrating upper spectrum treated as a physical condition: sustained tones extended, upper partials exposed and separated until the instrument's apparent singularity fragments. Fixed media — zurna
№ 03 2026 Intergrain Electroacoustic A continuous scraping gesture examined at a much smaller scale — a pick's ridge-by-ridge contact across a string, set against the güiro's related structure at another physical scale. Fixed media — bass/electric guitar string scrapes and güiro
№ 04 2026 Aerolith Electroacoustic An air-column instrument beside a resonating metal surface: bass-clarinet breath and multiphonics exchanged with a bowed cymbal's slowly transforming partials. Fixed media — bass clarinet and bowed cymbal
№ 05 2026 Craquelure Electroacoustic Two forms of stored energy — harp-string tension and ceramic brittleness — brought to a region where their behaviours become interchangeable. Fixed media — harp and ceramic/porcelain objects
№ 06 2026 Endobody Electroacoustic The body as an arrangement of internal spaces: vocal cavities and a bass drum's enclosed volume, working from their structural similarity. Fixed media — voice and bass drum
№ 07 2026 Remanence Electroacoustic What remains after a sound's apparent cause disappears: prepared-piano strings, muted attacks and soundboard resonance, with springs holding excitation that re-emerges later. Fixed media — prepared piano and metal springs
