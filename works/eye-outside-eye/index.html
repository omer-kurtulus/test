eye | outside | eye

A corpus built from Joëlle Léandre's double-bass and voice recordings made for Somax2 at IRCAM, navigated live by SoMax 2 against the performer in the room.

Year
2025
Category
AI & Machine Listening
Instrumentation
Voice and live interactive electronics, with a pre-recorded double-bass and voice corpus

eye | outside | eye ⟂⟂⟟𒅆𒇽𒅆 is written for voice and live interactive electronics, built in Max/MSP with SoMax 2 for pre-recorded corpus–system interaction. The corpus is constructed from double-bass and voice recordings by Joëlle Léandre, made for Somax2 during her residency at IRCAM; the composer performs the live voice and electronics layer.

The system does not accompany the performer. It listens, searches the archive of Léandre’s recorded playing, and answers with material she has already made — so what returns is recognisably hers, recombined by the system in real time.
