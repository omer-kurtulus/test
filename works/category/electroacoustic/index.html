Electroacoustic

Fixed media and instrument-plus-processing works, largely spatial: ambisonic and multichannel composition.

01 Electroacoustic / A Mechanics of Almost IV Aerolith An air-column instrument beside a resonating metal surface: bass-clarinet breath and multiphonics exchanged with a bowed cymbal's slowly transforming partials. 2026 Fixed media — bass clarinet and bowed cymbal
02 Electroacoustic / A Mechanics of Almost II Afterglare The zurna's penetrating upper spectrum treated as a physical condition: sustained tones extended, upper partials exposed and separated until the instrument's apparent singularity fragments. 2026 Fixed media — zurna
03 Electroacoustic / A Mechanics of Almost I Anaculaton Timpani reduced to impact → membrane → resonance → decay, then interfered with: attacks activate virtual resonating bodies beyond the instrument's scale, alongside wind, tunnels, machinery and distant environmental resonance. 2026 Fixed media — timpani and field recordings
04 Electroacoustic / A Mechanics of Almost V Craquelure Two forms of stored energy — harp-string tension and ceramic brittleness — brought to a region where their behaviours become interchangeable. 2026 Fixed media — harp and ceramic/porcelain objects
05 Electroacoustic / A Mechanics of Almost VI Endobody The body as an arrangement of internal spaces: vocal cavities and a bass drum's enclosed volume, working from their structural similarity. 2026 Fixed media — voice and bass drum
06 Electroacoustic / A Mechanics of Almost III Intergrain A continuous scraping gesture examined at a much smaller scale — a pick's ridge-by-ridge contact across a string, set against the güiro's related structure at another physical scale. 2026 Fixed media — bass/electric guitar string scrapes and güiro
07 Electroacoustic / A Mechanics of Almost VII Remanence What remains after a sound's apparent cause disappears: prepared-piano strings, muted attacks and soundboard resonance, with springs holding excitation that re-emerges later. 2026 Fixed media — prepared piano and metal springs
08 Electroacoustic an arche One cello, three performers, none of them sovereign over it. Bow pressure and contact — not pitch — are the ground, analysed live by FluCoMa and diffused into a quadraphonic ring in which no fixed origin can be located. 2026 One cello shared by three performers, quadraphonic diffusion, live electronics and lightMax/MSP · FluCoMa · Jitter
09 Electroacoustic Arundo_Trachea The oboe stripped of all melodic function and reduced to its physical reality: a vibrating double reed and a hollow wooden tube, heard from inside the bore. 2026 Oboe and electronics, fixed media8:25Spectral decomposition · Micro-acoustic isolation · Real-time DSP
