Electroacoustic

Fixed media and instrument-plus-processing works, largely spatial: ambisonic and multichannel composition.

01 Aerolith Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost IV 2026 An air-column instrument beside a resonating metal surface: bass-clarinet breath and multiphonics exchanged with a bowed cymbal's slowly transforming partials. Fixed media — bass clarinet and bowed cymbal
02 Afterglare Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost II 2026 The zurna's penetrating upper spectrum treated as a physical condition: sustained tones extended, upper partials exposed and separated until the instrument's apparent singularity fragments. Fixed media — zurna
03 Anaculaton Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost I 2026 Timpani reduced to impact → membrane → resonance → decay, then interfered with: attacks activate virtual resonating bodies beyond the instrument's scale, alongside wind, tunnels, machinery and distant environmental resonance. Fixed media — timpani and field recordings
04 Craquelure Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost V 2026 Two forms of stored energy — harp-string tension and ceramic brittleness — brought to a region where their behaviours become interchangeable. Fixed media — harp and ceramic/porcelain objects
05 Endobody Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost VI 2026 The body as an arrangement of internal spaces: vocal cavities and a bass drum's enclosed volume, working from their structural similarity. Fixed media — voice and bass drum
06 Intergrain Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost III 2026 A continuous scraping gesture examined at a much smaller scale — a pick's ridge-by-ridge contact across a string, set against the güiro's related structure at another physical scale. Fixed media — bass/electric guitar string scrapes and güiro
07 Remanence Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost VII 2026 What remains after a sound's apparent cause disappears: prepared-piano strings, muted attacks and soundboard resonance, with springs holding excitation that re-emerges later. Fixed media — prepared piano and metal springs
08 an arche Electroacoustic 2026 One cello, three performers, none of them sovereign over it. Bow pressure and contact — not pitch — are the ground, analysed live by FluCoMa and diffused into a quadraphonic ring in which no fixed origin can be located. One cello shared by three performers, quadraphonic diffusion, live electronics and lightMax/MSP · FluCoMa · Jitter
09 Arundo_Trachea Electroacoustic 2026 The oboe stripped of all melodic function and reduced to its physical reality: a vibrating double reed and a hollow wooden tube, heard from inside the bore. Oboe and electronics, fixed media8:25Spectral decomposition · Micro-acoustic isolation · Real-time DSP
10 STRATA Electroacoustic Strata I 2026 A single cut through the ground of Istanbul: every historical layer sounds from the first second, separated only by height. Tuned to a pitch set measured from the call to prayer in Hagia Sophia — which is never heard. Fixed media, 5th-order ambisonic (ambiX ACN/SN3D), 36 channels7:30Composers Desktop Project · Ableton Live · Python
