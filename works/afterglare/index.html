Works / Electroacoustic / A Mechanics of Almost II

Afterglare

The zurna's penetrating upper spectrum treated as a physical condition: sustained tones extended, upper partials exposed and separated until the instrument's apparent singularity fragments.

Year
2026
Category
Electroacoustic
Instrumentation
Fixed media — zurna

Afterglare begins with the unusually concentrated spectral energy of the zurna. Rather than suppressing its penetrating upper spectrum, the piece treats this sharpness as a physical condition capable of becoming unstable. Long sustained tones are extended beyond their expected temporal scale. Upper partials are exposed, prolonged and separated until the apparently singular instrumental sound fragments.

Second piece of A Mechanics of Almost.
