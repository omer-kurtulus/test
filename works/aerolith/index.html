Works / Electroacoustic / A Mechanics of Almost IV

Aerolith

An air-column instrument beside a resonating metal surface: bass-clarinet breath and multiphonics exchanged with a bowed cymbal's slowly transforming partials.

Year
2026
Category
Electroacoustic
Instrumentation
Fixed media — bass clarinet and bowed cymbal

Aerolith places an air-column instrument beside a resonating metal surface. The bass clarinet is approached through breath, air tones, unstable low frequencies, multiphonics and the changing resonances of a tube. The cymbal begins from another condition: friction activates a rigid metallic surface whose partials may continue long after the original gesture.

Fourth piece of A Mechanics of Almost.
