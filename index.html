Anaculaton Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost I 2026 Timpani reduced to impact → membrane → resonance → decay, then interfered with: attacks activate virtual resonating bodies beyond the instrument's scale, alongside wind, tunnels, machinery and distant environmental resonance. Fixed media — timpani and field recordings
Craquelure Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost V 2026 Two forms of stored energy — harp-string tension and ceramic brittleness — brought to a region where their behaviours become interchangeable. Fixed media — harp and ceramic/porcelain objects
Endobody Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost VI 2026 The body as an arrangement of internal spaces: vocal cavities and a bass drum's enclosed volume, working from their structural similarity. Fixed media — voice and bass drum
Intergrain Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost III 2026 A continuous scraping gesture examined at a much smaller scale — a pick's ridge-by-ridge contact across a string, set against the güiro's related structure at another physical scale. Fixed media — bass/electric guitar string scrapes and güiro
Remanence Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost VII 2026 What remains after a sound's apparent cause disappears: prepared-piano strings, muted attacks and soundboard resonance, with springs holding excitation that re-emerges later. Fixed media — prepared piano and metal springs
