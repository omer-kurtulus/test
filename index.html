15 Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost III 2026 Intergrain A continuous scraping gesture examined at a much smaller scale — a pick's ridge-by-ridge contact across a string, set against the güiro's related structure at another physical scale. Fixed media — bass/electric guitar string scrapes and güiro
16 Electroacoustic A Mechanics of Almost VII 2026 Remanence What remains after a sound's apparent cause disappears: prepared-piano strings, muted attacks and soundboard resonance, with springs holding excitation that re-emerges later. Fixed media — prepared piano and metal springs
