Contact

Write to me about any of these — a short message is enough, and every message is read:

  • Commissions — new works for soloists, ensembles and orchestra, with or without electronics.
  • Scores and performance materials — perusal scores, parts and technical riders for any work in the catalogue.
  • Recordings and multichannel masters — multichannel and ambisonic works are available as full-resolution masters, and decodes can be prepared for a specific loudspeaker layout.
  • Collaborations — live electronics and machine listening (Max/MSP, Somax2, FluCoMa), corpus-based work with performers, technical documentation.

If you are writing about a particular piece, its title in the subject helps — every work page has a ready-made message for that.

The Agora of New Music

The Agora of New Music is a free meeting place for composers and performers of contemporary music, which I started in 2026: performers find new works to play, and composers find people to play theirs — with no prizes, no juries and no entry fees. If you would like to know more about it, the Agora is always glad to hear from you at info@theagoraofnewmusic.com.
